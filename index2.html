      Artist / Researcher / Curator

      Martin Wöllenstein is a transdisciplinary artist and researcher. His work investigates global infrastructures, histories, and politics to trace how material and symbolic violence is encoded into systems. In mostly collaborative projects, he explores how to counteract these systems by proposing more liveable and joyful worlds. His methods include co-design, space-making, writing, curating, and publishing.

      Martin studied Fine Arts, Scenography, and Transversal Design in Halle, Havana, and Basel. He co-founded the artist platforms Nnoboa Space in Ghana (2023) and VIVA Comunidad Creativa in Cuba (2017) and is a frequent collaborator of the Berlin-based, zero-waste fashion label IMPARI.

      His work has been shown internationally at institutions including Havana Biennial (2015), Nubuke Foundation Accra (2023), Villa Medici Rome (2024), Artco Gallery Berlin (2024), Goethe Institute Accra (2025), Perve Gallery Lisbon (2025), and CIVIC Basel (2025). He lives between Basel and Accra.
